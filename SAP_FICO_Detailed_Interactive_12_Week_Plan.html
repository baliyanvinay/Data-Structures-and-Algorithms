Weeks 1-2: Accounting + SAP Foundations

Learn these first

  • Debit / Credit
  • Assets, Liabilities and Equity
  • Revenue vs Expense
  • Balance Sheet
  • Profit & Loss statement
  • Trial Balance
  • Journal entries
  • Fiscal year and accounting periods
  • Accruals
  • Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable

Business processes

  • Procure-to-Pay (P2P)
  • Order-to-Cash (O2C)
  • Record-to-Report (R2R)

Worked example

Laptop purchased on credit for ₹1,00,000 Dr Computer Equipment ₹1,00,000 Cr Vendor Payable ₹1,00,000

Core concepts

  • ERP and SAP S/4HANA overview
  • SAP GUI vs SAP Fiori
  • Client, Company and Company Code
  • Plant, Purchasing Organization and Sales Organization
  • Chart of Accounts and GL Accounts
  • Fiscal Year Variant
  • Posting Period Variant
  • Business Area, Segment and Profit Center

Key relationship

Company Code ↓ Chart of Accounts ↓ GL Accounts ↓ Accounting Documents

Weeks 3-7: Financial Accounting (FI)

Topics

  • Chart of Accounts and GL master
  • Account groups
  • Document types and number ranges
  • Posting keys and field status
  • Currencies and ledgers
  • Document posting and reversal
  • Clearing and open-item management
  • Financial statements

Practice

Rent paid: ₹50,000 Dr Rent Expense ₹50,000 Cr Bank ₹50,000

Post the entry and inspect document header, line items, posting keys and ledger impact.

Topics

  • Supplier / Business Partner
  • Supplier invoice and credit memo
  • Outgoing payments
  • Automatic Payment Program
  • Payment terms and cash discounts
  • Supplier clearing and aging
  • Special G/L and down payments

End-to-end flow

Purchase Order → Goods Receipt → Invoice Receipt → Supplier Payment

Focus on MM-FI touchpoints and the accounting entries at each stage.

Topics

  • Customer / Business Partner
  • Customer invoices and credit memos
  • Incoming payments and clearing
  • Partial and residual payments
  • Dunning and customer aging
  • Customer down payments

SD-FI flow

Sales Order → Delivery → Billing → FI Document → Customer Payment

Topics

  • Asset classes and asset master
  • Chart of depreciation and depreciation areas
  • Asset acquisition and transfer
  • Retirement and sale
  • Depreciation
  • Asset under construction

Worked example

Machine purchase: ₹10,00,000 Useful life: 5 years Dr Asset ₹10,00,000 Cr Vendor ₹10,00,000 Monthly depreciation: Dr Depreciation Expense Cr Accumulated Depreciation

Bank topics

  • House banks and bank accounts
  • Electronic bank statement basics
  • Bank reconciliation

Closing topics

  • Foreign currency valuation
  • GR/IR clearing
  • Accruals and recurring entries
  • Period-end processes
  • Balance carry-forward
  • Financial statements and closing cockpit
Checkpoint: Explain what happens in SAP Finance from invoice creation through month-end close.

Mini implementation

Company → Company Code → Chart of Accounts → Fiscal Year → GL Accounts → Customers / Vendors → Cost Centers → Profit Centers → Assets

Run the complete cycle

  • Introduce capital
  • Purchase raw material
  • Post supplier invoice and payment
  • Post customer sale and receipt
  • Purchase an asset and run depreciation
  • Post expenses and allocations
  • Perform month-end close
  • Produce P&L and Balance Sheet
